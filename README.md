## 🚀 Latest projects

### [Study Assistant (RAG)](https://github.com/KalyanKolapalli/Study_Assistent_Rag)
A local study assistant for lecture PDFs. Upload your lecture slides and ask questions about them. It finds the relevant parts of your notes and answers from them, and everything runs on your own machine.
- **Stack:** Python, Streamlit, Ollama, ChromaDB

### [Eye Contact Monitor](https://github.com/KalyanKolapalli/eye-contact-monitor)
A Python webcam app that tracks your gaze and head position to show whether you're keeping eye contact. It also recognises hand gestures, which makes it useful for practising presentations, interviews, and video calls.
- **Stack:** Python, webcam computer vision, gaze/head tracking, hand-gesture detection
